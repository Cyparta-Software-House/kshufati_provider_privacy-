Privacy Policy — كشوفاتي (Kshufati) Provider
App: كشوفاتي Provider (Android package com.cyparta.kshufatiProvider)
Last updated: 15 September 2026

Kshufati Provider ("the App", "we", "us") is a booking and appointment management app. Service providers use it to receive booking requests, organize their schedule, manage their appointments, and track their earnings.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect
Account Information
The phone number and password you use to register, log in, and recover your account.
Profile details you provide, such as your name, the services you offer, the areas you cover, and your working hours.
Documents you upload to verify your account, such as professional certificates or licenses.
Booking & Appointment Information
Booking requests you receive, accept, or decline.
Your schedule: upcoming, rescheduled, and completed appointments, with their date, time, and address.
Basic client details needed to fulfill a booking, such as the client's name and the appointment address.
Service notes or reports you attach to a completed appointment. These may describe the service you provided to the client.
Payment & Earnings Information
Appointment prices, payments received, wallet balance, transaction history, and withdrawal requests.
Location Information
Approximate and precise location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION). We use it to show you nearby booking requests, estimate the distance to each appointment, and display your coverage area.
We collect location only while you are using the App, and only after you grant permission on your device.
Device & Technical Information
A push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), used to notify you about new bookings, schedule changes, and account activity.
Basic device and connection information needed to run the App and communicate with our servers.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:

Create, verify, and secure your account.
Send you booking requests that match your location, services, and availability.
Organize your schedule and let you accept, reschedule, start, and complete appointments.
Record completed appointments and any notes or reports attached to them.
Calculate your earnings and process payments and withdrawals.
Send you notifications about bookings, reminders, and your account.
Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
Meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
3. Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

With clients who book you: they see your profile and the details of their appointment with you.
With service providers who help us run the App, such as cloud hosting and Firebase for notifications. They must keep the information confidential.
When required by law, or when needed to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public.
4. Data Retention
We keep your information while your account is active, and for as long as we need it to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Security
We protect your information with technical and organizational measures, including encrypting data in transit to our servers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Permissions Used
Permission	Purpose
Internet	Connect to our servers to load and sync your bookings and schedule.
Precise & approximate location	Show nearby booking requests and the distance to appointments.
Notifications	Alert you about new bookings, schedule changes, and reminders.
Camera, photos & files	Upload verification documents and attach reports to appointments.
7. Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights or to delete your account, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy
The App is for professional service providers. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

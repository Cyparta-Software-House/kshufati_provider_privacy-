# Privacy Policy — كشوفاتي (Kshufati) Provider

**App:** كشوفاتي Provider (Android package `com.kshufati.kshufati_provider`)
**Last updated:** 29 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the كشوفاتي Provider application ("the App", "we", "us")
collects, uses, and protects your information when you use it as a healthcare service
provider. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account & Identity
- **Phone number** and **password** used to register, log in, and recover your account.
- **Profile details** you provide: name, professional information, areas of coverage,
  and availability.
- **Documents** you upload: professional certificates, licenses, and related images or
  files.

### Health & Service Data
- **Visit and request data**, including scheduled visits, patient requests you accept or
  reject, and **medical reports** you submit through the App.

### Financial Data
- **Wallet and payment information**, including transaction history and withdrawal
  requests, used to process and display your earnings.

### Location Data
- **Approximate and precise location** (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`,
  `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) used to match you with nearby visits and to display
  service coverage. Location is collected while you use the App in line with your
  device permissions.

### Device & Technical Data
- **Push notification token** (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver notifications
  about visits, requests, and account activity.
- Basic device and connectivity information required to operate the App and communicate
  with our servers over the Internet (`INTERNET`).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and secure your provider account and authenticate you.
- Match you with patient visits and requests based on your location and availability.
- Process visits, medical reports, payments, and withdrawal requests.
- Send you service-related notifications.
- Verify your professional certificates and licenses.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information:
- With **patients/clients** as necessary to fulfill visits and requests (e.g. your
  professional profile and the relevant visit details).
- With **service providers** that support our operations (e.g. cloud hosting, Firebase
  for push notifications), bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- When **required by law**, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety,
  and security of users and the public.

---

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide
the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
You may request deletion of your account and associated data (see Section 7).

---

## 5. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including
encrypted transmission to our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | Communicate with our servers and APIs. |
| Precise & approximate location | Match you with nearby visits and show coverage areas. |
| Notifications (FCM) | Deliver visit, request, and account notifications. |
| Photos / files | Let you upload certificates, licenses, and documents. |

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your
personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights or to request account
deletion, contact us using the details below.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by professional healthcare providers and is not directed to
children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
